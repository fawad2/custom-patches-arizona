Built for year-round seasons

Arizona soccer runs nearly all year, with fall and spring club seasons, school teams and winter tournaments. Patches have to survive heat, sweat and a lot of washing. Embroidered and woven crests stay sharp, and PVC holds up on bags and outdoor gear.

Number patches come in custom fonts and colors to match your kit.

Options

Patch types

Embroidered, PVC, woven, chenille, printed and leather. We will recommend one based on where the patch is worn.

Numbers

Custom fonts and colors.

Iron-on

Attaches with a household iron in under a minute.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.
