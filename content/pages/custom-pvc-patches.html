Questions

PVC Patches FAQ

What is a PVC patch?

A patch molded from soft polyvinyl chloride. It is waterproof, flexible and reproduces fine lines and small text that thread cannot hold at small sizes.

Are PVC patches waterproof and washable?

Yes. They do not absorb water and can go through the washing machine. Wash cold and inside out if the patch is attached with adhesive.

Will they fade in the Arizona sun?

PVC is UV-resistant and the color runs through each layer, so it does not fade, fray, crack or peel under normal outdoor use.

Can I iron on a PVC patch?

Not with a household iron directly on the patch. Use hook-and-loop, sew-on or adhesive backing, or a heat press with fabric adhesive at 160°C / 320°F for 20–30 seconds.

How thick are they?

Standard patches are about 2 mm, with raised design elements around 0.7 mm. We also make 3 mm patches for tactical and heavy-fabric use.

Is there a mold fee?

No. Tooling is included in the patch price, and reorders of the same design get 25% off per piece.

Can I get a physical sample before a big order?

Yes. Every order gets a free digital proof, and large orders can get a pre-production sample; the cost depends on order size.

What is the difference between 2D and 3D PVC?

2D has flat layers and costs less. 3D adds sculpted depth for a premium feel.
