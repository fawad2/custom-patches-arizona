Letter season, Arizona style

Arizona letterman jackets come out for a short winter, which means they spend most of the year in a closet and need patches that look new every season. We test chenille through 100 washes with no fraying or color loss, and we recommend sew-on or iron-on with a double-felt base for heavyweight jackets.

Athletic departments, booster clubs and award programs order letters, sport icons and year bars together so every piece matches the school colors. Tell us your award night and we will build the schedule backward from it.

Upgrades

Embroidery + chenille

Stitched outlines, mascots or small text on top of the chenille for a vintage varsity look.

Double felt

A thicker, sturdier base that holds shape on heavy jackets and carries intricate details.

Split backing

Mix backings in one order, such as iron-on letters and sew-on mascots.

Backings

Iron-on

The most popular choice for varsity jackets. Press at 285°F after a 4–5 second pre-press of the garment.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Available, but not recommended for chenille because the loop side has to be sewn to the jacket anyway.

Questions

Chenille Patches FAQ

What does chenille mean?

Chenille is French for caterpillar, after the fuzzy, raised yarn texture.

How are chenille patches made?

Yarn is looped in layers onto a felt base, with short lengths caught between longer strands to build the raised pile.

Are they iron-on?

Yes. Iron-on chenille lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press at 285°F. For heavy letterman jackets, sew-on is the most permanent.

Can I wash chenille patches?

Yes. Wash cold, inside out, with mild detergent. Our patches hold up through 100 test washes without fraying.

Why does chenille cost more than embroidery?

It needs dedicated chenille machines and more material per patch. In return it has the premium look buyers associate with varsity gear.

Chenille or embroidered?

Chenille suits bold, simple shapes with large fill areas. Embroidery suits fine detail and small text. Many letters combine both.

Can I mix backings in one order?

Yes. Note the split on your quote request.
