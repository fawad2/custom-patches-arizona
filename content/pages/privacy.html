Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Custom Patches Arizona (“we”) collects through this website and how it is used.

What we collect

When you request a quote we collect the details you enter: your name, email, optional phone number and organization, the products and quantities you ask about, and any artwork you upload. Our website host also records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare quotes and mockups, produce and ship your order, and reply to your messages. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to show more relevant ads on other websites. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to opt out of this kind of sharing. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as an opt-out request for that browser.

Your artwork

Artwork you send is used only to quote and make your order. You keep all rights to it, and we keep approved files on record so reorders match.

Retention and security

We keep quote and order records for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal and accounting requirements, and we protect them with reasonable safeguards.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
