In Arizona

Desert hockey is real hockey

Arizona has a busy youth and adult hockey scene across the Valley, Tucson and Flagstaff rinks. Teams need jersey crests, C and A patches, and tournament patches on short notice, and parents want matching gear for the stands.

Sew-on or Velcro holds best on jerseys that go through a washer after every game; iron-on works well on hoodies and jackets.

Options

Patch types

Embroidered, PVC, woven, chenille, printed and leather. We will recommend one based on where the patch is worn.

Names and numbers

Player names, numbers and team logos.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.
