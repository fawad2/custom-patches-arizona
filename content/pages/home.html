Questions

Custom patch FAQ

Still deciding? Send your artwork and we will recommend a patch type with your free quote.

What types of custom patches do you make?

Embroidered, PVC, chenille, woven, printed (sublimated), leather, sequin, silicone labels and hook-and-loop patches, plus keychains and PVC shoe charms.

How do I apply an iron-on patch?

A heat press gives the strongest bond: 160°C / 320°F for 15–20 seconds for most patches, then let it cool. With a household iron, set it for your fabric, cover the patch with a thin cloth and press firmly for 20–30 seconds. See the iron-on guide for each patch type.

Can I wash clothes with patches on them?

Yes. Wash inside out in cold water and air dry. Embroidered and PVC patches handle about 50 washes this way; sewn-on patches last the longest.

Can patches be removed later?

Sew-on patches come off by cutting the stitches. Iron-on patches can be loosened with heat and peeled, though some adhesive may remain. Velcro patches simply pull off.

Is there a minimum order?

Most patches start at 5 pieces. A few products have their own minimums, like 50 keychains or 100 shoe charms per design. The per-piece price drops as quantity goes up.

What sizes and shapes can I order?

Any shape. Common sizes are 2.25–2.5 in for caps, 4–5 in for the left chest, 10–12 in across the chest and 12–14 in for jacket backs.

Do you make patches for hats?

Yes. Hat patches are sized to curve with the crown, in embroidery, leather, PVC or woven, with iron-on, sew-on or Velcro backing.

How long does an order take?

Most orders arrive 7–14 business days after you approve the proof. Rush production is available; tell us your date before you order so we can confirm it.

Do I see a proof first?

Always. Every order gets a free digital proof with unlimited revisions, and nothing is produced until you approve it.

Which backing should I choose?

Sew-on for the most permanent hold, iron-on for quick near-permanent application, Velcro for patches you swap, and peel-and-stick for one-day use.

Do you take wholesale and resale orders?

Yes. Brands, decorators and distributors get monthly volume pricing, designs kept on file and white-label packaging.

Who orders custom patches in Arizona?

Work crews and service companies, fire and EMS departments, schools and booster clubs, sports teams, riding clubs, hat and apparel brands, breweries and restaurants, scouts, and families.

Will my bulk order look consistent?

Yes. Approved files and thread colors are kept on file and every run is inspected against the approved proof, so patch 5,000 matches patch 1.

Do you ship everywhere in Arizona?

Yes. Shipping is free and tracked to any Arizona address, from Phoenix and Tucson to Flagstaff, Yuma and small towns in between.
