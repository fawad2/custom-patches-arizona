About us

A patch partner for Arizona

Custom Patches Arizona makes custom patches to order for the teams, crews, clubs, schools and brands of one state. Behind it is a team with two decades in embroidery and patch production, from digitizing and machine work to finishing and quality control.

A patch carries identity: a crew, a unit, a school, a club, a brand. We take that seriously. You get a real mockup before anything is made, honest advice about which patch type will last where you plan to wear it, and the same inspection on a 5-piece order as on a 5,000-piece run.

How we work

What you can count on

Craft first

Every design is digitized for your patch type and checked against your approved proof before it ships.

Small orders welcome

Five patches for a family reunion get the same care as five thousand for a uniform program.

Our patches are made in our own factory, where more than half of the production team are women.

Less waste

The process

Sketch, mockup, patch

  1. 01

    Send your design

    Upload a logo, a photo or a napkin sketch with your size, quantity and backing. No artwork? Describe it and we draw it.

  2. 02

    Approve a free proof

    We send a digital mockup with colors, size and stitch count. Revise it as many times as you like.

  3. 03

    We make your patches

    Production starts only after you approve. Rush production is available for hard deadlines.

  4. 04

    Delivered in Arizona

    Every order is quality-checked and shipped free with tracking to any Arizona address, usually 7–14 business days after approval.
