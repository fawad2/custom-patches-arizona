Gym season gear

Arizona basketball season lands in the cooler months, which is when hoodies, warm-ups and jackets come out. Schools, club programs and tournament organizers order matching patches for players, coaches and families.

Embroidery uses weather-resistant thread and bold colors; chenille gives the letterman look for award patches.

Options

Patch types

Embroidered, PVC, woven, chenille, printed and leather. We will recommend one based on where the patch is worn.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.
