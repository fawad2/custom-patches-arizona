Borders

Embroidered border

A satin-stitched frame in any thickness. Works on custom die-cut shapes where a merrow edge cannot follow.

Merrow border

A thick overlocked edge that stops fraying. Best on circles, ovals, squares and other simple shapes.

Laser-cut border

Heat-sealed, cut to the outline of the design for detailed shapes.

No border

A raw, clean edge for a modern or DIY look.

Backings

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.

Button loop

A small loop on the back so the patch hangs from a button or pin without sewing.

Thread and finish upgrades

Metallic thread

Gold, silver and colored metallics for emblems that need to catch light.

Glow-in-the-dark thread

Charges under light and glows afterward. Popular for night events and safety details.

3D puff embroidery

Raised stitching over foam for bold lettering on caps and sportswear.

Questions

Embroidered Patches FAQ

What does it cost to use more than 9 colors?

The first 9 thread colors are free. You can go up to 12; colors 10–12 add a small charge that we show on your quote before you approve anything.

What is the difference between embroidered and woven patches?

Embroidered patches are stitched onto a fabric base, which gives raised texture. Woven patches are made by interlacing fine threads on a loom, which gives a flatter patch that holds smaller text and finer detail.

How long does an embroidered patch order take?

Usually 7–14 business days from proof approval to delivery at your Arizona address. Rush production is available; tell us your date when you ask for a quote.

What fabric do you use?

Cotton-rich twill is the standard base because it is soft, light and stitches cleanly. Polyester, wool and other bases are available for special looks.

How much of the patch is embroidered?

Typical coverage is 75–85%. Anything up to 99% costs the same. Full 100% coverage costs a little more, and we show you a preview so you can compare.

How long do embroidered patches last?

Two to three years of regular wear and washing is normal, and much longer with cold washes and air drying.

What sizes work best?

Cap fronts take 2.25–2.5 in, left chest 4–5 in, across the chest 10–12 in and jacket backs 12–14 in. Any custom size is possible.
