In Arizona

For the few cold months, and the rest of the year

Jacket season is short in Phoenix and long in Flagstaff, and jackets with good patches get worn in both. Brands use back patches for limited drops, schools for letterman jackets, and clubs for colors.

Pick chenille for varsity and denim, leather for biker and western styles, and embroidery for everyday branded jackets.

Options

Embroidered

Detailed designs for everyday and branded jackets.

Chenille

Classic letterman look, including oversized high-pile patches.

Leather

For leather, biker and western jackets.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.
