Know what your bottom rocker says

In motorcycle culture a bottom rocker that names a state or city can read as a territory claim. If you are not part of an established club, avoid a design that pairs a club-style name with "Arizona" or a city on the bottom. Riding groups, charities and businesses often use a slogan, a year or a chapter number instead.

Questions

Rocker Patches FAQ

What is a rocker?

A curved banner patch worn above or below a larger back patch.

Can anyone wear a bottom rocker?

Many riders avoid territory-style bottom rockers unless they belong to the group, because they can read as claiming an area.

What size should I choose?

Match the rocker width to your back patch and panel; 11 in is the adult standard.

What file format do you need?

PNG, PDF or AI work best. For text-only rockers, send the wording and font choice.

How long does production take?

About 8 business days after approval for around 100 pieces. Larger runs take longer.
