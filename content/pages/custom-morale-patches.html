Build it

Material

PVC for outdoor durability and fine detail, embroidery for a classic look.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.

Questions

Morale Patches FAQ

What are morale patches used for?

Building team spirit, marking events and sharing inside jokes. Most are PVC or embroidered for durability.

How do I design one?

Pick PVC or embroidery, send artwork or an idea, and choose a backing. We send a free mockup.

Which backing is best?

Velcro to swap patches, iron-on for permanent wear, adhesive for temporary use.

Can I order for my whole unit?

Yes. Larger orders get lower per-piece pricing.
