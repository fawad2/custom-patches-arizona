Questions

Motorcycle Club Patches FAQ

What is in a full set?

Six pieces designed together with matched borders, threads and backing, approved in one digital proof.

What is the minimum?

One per design on full sets. Standalone patches have a 5-piece minimum.

Sew-on or iron-on?

Sew-on for leather and denim. Iron-on can loosen from flexing, heat and rain.

Can later orders match?

Yes. We store your digitized files so thread, border and backing match on every reorder.

Top rocker or bottom rocker?

The top rocker sits above the center patch with the club name; the bottom rocker sits below with territory or chapter.
