Questions

PVC Shoe Charms FAQ

How do I add charms to clogs?

Hold the top strap and push the charm base through a hole from the outside.

How do I remove them without damage?

Push from the inside while gently twisting and lifting from the edges. Do not pull from the outside.

Can I mix designs in one order?

No. Each design needs its own mold, so each is a separate order.

Can I order fewer than 100?

Yes, but the price is the same as 100, so most customers order at least 100.

Is there a mold fee?

No. Mold, production and shipping are included.

Are they waterproof?

Yes. PVC resists water and scratches, so they hold up outdoors.
