Patch types

Embroidered

High-density stitching on twill. Best for uniforms, MC vests and denim.

PVC

Waterproof and heat resistant. Best for outdoor crews and tactical gear.

Chenille

Raised varsity texture for teams and letterman jackets.

Backings

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.

Name Patches FAQ

Which material should I choose?

Embroidered twill for leather and denim, woven for fine text, PVC for waterproof outdoor use, chenille for a premium varsity finish.

Can I see it before production?

Yes. You get a digital proof with unlimited revisions.

What shapes and finishes are available?

Circle, square, shield, rocker and die-cut, with merrowed or heat-cut edges, 3D puff, and matte or glossy PVC.

Do you make name patches for hats?

Yes, in hat sizes that curve well and stay low-profile.
