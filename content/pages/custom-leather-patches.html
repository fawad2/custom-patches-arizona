In Arizona

A natural fit for Arizona western and hat brands

Leather patches belong on the gear Arizona actually wears: trucker caps, ranch jackets, waxed aprons, canvas bags and boots. A typical hat-brand build is genuine tan leather with a laser-engraved logo, 2 in for snapbacks and 3 in for wide-front truckers, with iron-on backing pre-applied so the patches go straight onto a heat press.

Dry desert air is hard on leather. Keep strong soaps and detergents off the patch, use a leather cleaner, and avoid leaving pressed leather above 200°C, which damages it permanently.

Materials

Faux leather

PU with consistent color and finish. The bulk branding and merch choice.

Genuine leather

100% genuine split leather with natural grain that develops character over time.

Embroidered leather-backed

Embroidery stitched onto a leather base. Popular with fashion brands and riding clubs.

Finishes

Laser engraved

A sharp single-color burn that contrasts with the leather.

Debossed

Pressed into the surface for depth.

Embossed

Raised from the surface on genuine leather.

Photo print

Full-color print on leather with iron-on backing.

Backings

Iron-on

Press with a heat press at 140°C / 284°F for 15–20 seconds with adhesive. Pre-applied backing is available for hat makers.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.

Questions

Leather Patches FAQ

What are the most popular shapes for leather hat patches?

Circle, rectangle, oval, hexagon and shield. Rectangles and hexagons sit best on trucker and snapback front panels.

What size should a leather hat patch be?

Most are 2–3.5 in wide, depending on the hat and how much detail is in the logo.

How do I attach leather patches?

Sewing is the most secure. Iron-on with a heat press is reliable too, and pre-applied backing is available for hat production.

How is my logo put on the leather?

Usually by laser engraving, which burns a sharp single-color image into the surface. Debossing and embossing are also available.

Can I wash clothes with leather patches?

Yes. Hand wash in lukewarm water and dry flat so the patch stays aligned.

Faux, genuine or embroidered leather?

Faux ($2.50) is consistent and economical in bulk. Genuine ($5.00) has natural grain and ages well. Embroidered leather-backed ($8.00) is the richest look.

Do you supply hat brands in bulk?

Yes, from 50 pieces to 10,000+, with factory-ready iron-on backing that works on standard heat presses.
