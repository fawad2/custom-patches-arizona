Options

Designs

Bingo balls, numbered game designs and club patches.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Bingo Patches FAQ

Can I order just one?

The minimum is 10 per design.

How do I apply iron-on?

Press with a hot iron for 20–30 seconds through a thin cloth. Sew the edges for extra hold.

Can I add our club name?

Yes, and 10 or more per design lowers the per-piece price.
