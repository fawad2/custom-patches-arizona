Built for gym wear and outdoor gear

Silicone labels are a favorite of Arizona athletic and outdoor brands because they are soft against the skin, waterproof and do not crack when the garment stretches. They work on performance tees, running shorts, jackets and bags.

They tolerate both heat and cold, which suits gear that goes from a 110° trailhead to an air-conditioned car.

How they apply

Self-adhesive backing

The label carries its own adhesive.

Heat press

Press at 160°C / 320°F under a clean cloth for about 15 seconds.

Questions

Silicone Labels FAQ

What are silicone labels?

Raised, rubber-like labels made from silicone polymer that tolerate hot and cold temperatures.

How are they attached?

With a heat press. The self-adhesive backing bonds at 160°C / 320°F in about 15 seconds.

Are they safe on clothing?

Yes. They are soft and gentle against skin.

Are they waterproof?

Yes, they handle sweat and rain well. Long submersion or a poorly sealed edge can let water under, so press firmly.

Are they better for the environment than PVC?

They are not fully biodegradable, but silicone is generally considered a better choice than PVC, lasts a long time and is recyclable.
