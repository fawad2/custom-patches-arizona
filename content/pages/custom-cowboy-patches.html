Arizona is still cowboy country

Rodeos, ranch outfits, western wear shops and trail-riding groups across Arizona put patches on hats, vests and jackets. Steer skulls and western stars are classics; ranch brands and rodeo logos make the best custom orders.

For iron-on application, press through a thin cloth for 20–30 seconds and let it cool. On denim and vests, stitch the edges for extra hold.

Options

Designs

Cowboy hat, steer skull, western star or your artwork.

Formats

Embroidered, chenille, PVC, woven, printed or leather.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Questions

Cowboy & Western Patches FAQ

What is the minimum order?

10 pieces per design.

Do you make skull patches for vests?

Yes, large back patches and smaller jacket and bag patches.

How do I apply iron-on patches?

Press with a hot iron through a thin cloth for 20–30 seconds and let it cool. Sew the edges on denim or vests.

Do you offer bulk orders for rodeos and clubs?

Yes. Larger orders lower the per-patch cost.
