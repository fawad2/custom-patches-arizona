The most important accessory in Arizona

In a state where a hat is sun protection, cap patches see more wear than any other patch. Leather and PVC shrug off sweat; embroidery gives the classic textured look; woven holds the finest detail.

For hat brands and shops we supply patches with pre-applied iron-on backing that runs straight through a hat press.

Options

Materials

Embroidered, leather, PVC and woven are the most common.

Sew-on

Stitched through the garment. The most permanent hold, and the right call for leather, denim and heavy jackets.

Iron-on

Heat-activated adhesive for a near-permanent bond that lasts 50+ washes when applied with a heat press.

Velcro (hook & loop)

Hook side on the patch, loop side on the gear. Swap patches in seconds on uniforms, packs and caps.

Peel & stick

Temporary adhesive for events, costumes and one-day wear. No sewing or heat.

Questions

Hat Patches FAQ

What material is best for hats?

Embroidery for a classic look, PVC and leather for a modern, durable look, woven for detailed designs.

How long do hat patches last?

Good embroidered or PVC patches handle heavy wear and about 50 washes with cold water and air drying.

Do iron-on patches work on trucker hats?

Yes. They apply with a household iron or hat press and hold well.

Can I get names or initials?

Yes, embroidered names, initials and monograms.
