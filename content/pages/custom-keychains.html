Questions

Keychains FAQ

Why are simple shapes cheaper?

Circles and squares use simpler molds, which lowers production cost.

Can I print on both sides?

Yes, choose single or double-sided.

Can I order fewer than 50?

Yes, but the factory minimum is 50, so smaller orders cost the same as 50.

Can I see a physical sample first?

We send a digital draft for approval instead. For large orders, ask us about sample options.

Can the design print to the edge?

No. Leave a border of at least 1/32 in (0.03125 in).

Can I reuse the same mold later?

Yes. Once paid for, the stamp or mold can be reused for future orders and other products.
